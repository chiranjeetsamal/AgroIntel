    BEHIND THE OUTPUT

    Built to be inspected.

    01

    Two tasks, two datasets

    2,200 crop records describe soil and climate. Approximately 51,700 cleaned regional records describe historical yield. They are not joined as observations from the same farm.

    02

    Test data stays separate

    Stratified crop testing and chronological yield testing. Extra Trees models selected against baselines; classifier probabilities calibrated on training data. Production never enters the yield prediction inputs.

    03

    Explain, without overclaiming

    Local sensitivity replaces one input at a time with its training median. It shows how this particular prediction changes—not causality, and not additive SHAP attribution.

    Evaluation, sources & limitations

    Crop benchmark ↗ · Regional snapshot ↗

    The regional snapshot is community-maintained and attributed to government records. Historical yield estimates do not capture present-day management, irrigation, pests or changing climate. Live weather is not part of the trained yield model.
